Get started

From screenshots to team maps in three steps

Take screenshots of the entire roster for Task Force A, Task Force B, or both. Upload them through the Media Upload button and the app will identify each team's players and suggest Phase 1 and Phase 2 assignments. You can adjust the assignments before creating your maps.

  1. 1
    Capture your rosterScreenshot every section of the team roster. Keep the Task Force A or Task Force B heading visible on each image so the app knows which team it belongs to.
  2. 2
    Upload and scanTap Media Upload at the top, select Storm Roster, and submit your screenshots or recording. The app reads player names, roles, power and status, groups players by team, and creates suggested assignments.
  3. 3
    Adjust and generateReview the roster, edit or swap assignments as needed, then click Generate Map for a Phase 1 and Phase 2 image you can download and share.

Scan screenshots to see Team A and Team B tables, with Phase 1 and Phase 2 assignment totals.

Role means the in-game R1–R5 section, while Rank is the player's numbered position in their team roster. Click the pencil at the far left of a row to edit team, role, name, power, or status; click the save icon when finished. When you leave a changed row, choose whether to save or discard. Corrected OCR names are remembered in the editable dictionary. The left blue letter marks Participant; the right marks Sub. Each team roster groups Participants first, Subs second, and Needs review last, with highest to lowest power inside each group. Auto assignments are based on the power ranking of eligible participants within each team. The four strongest participants are Free Floaters in Phase 1 and take Nuclear Silo in Phase 2. In Phase 2, the two players who would hold Field Hospital 3 in Phase 1 instead go to Arsenal (stronger) and Merc. Factory (other). Subs, unknown statuses, and eligible players beyond 20 start Unassigned. Phase 1 and Phase 2 can be edited independently. Counts include everyone, even when filtered.
